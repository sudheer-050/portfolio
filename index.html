Scene 01 — About

From the plant floor
to the data stack

We combine process-engineering discipline with modern machine learning to turn messy, high-stakes data into decisions, models, and shipped AI products.

I started as a chemical process engineer at Hetero Labs, where I spent two years in a GMP pharmaceutical plant learning to find root causes in noisy, high-stakes production data — tracing yield loss back through batch records, tightening process controls, and training a team to trust the data over gut feel. That job taught me discipline under real operational pressure; it also showed me I wanted to build the analytical tools, not just consume them.

That pivot led me to a Data Analytics internship at Unified Mentor and an MS in Business Analytics at DePaul University's Kellstadt Graduate School of Business (GPA 3.925), graduating June 2026. Since then I've been deliberately pushing further into Machine Learning Engineering — completing both the Stanford Machine Learning Specialization and the DeepLearning.AI Deep Learning Specialization, while applying it to capstone and independent projects rather than leaving it in a notebook.

Today I work across the full stack end-to-end: SQL and Python for the data layer, scikit-learn/XGBoost/TensorFlow and LLM agents for the intelligence layer, Power BI/Tableau for the story, and Node.js, FastAPI, Docker, and Nginx when the answer is "build and deploy the product." Outside coursework, I ship independent projects to real infrastructure — a published PyPI package, a production chat app serving real users, and a self-hosted AI agent platform — because the fastest way to prove I can do the job is to already be doing it.

I'm currently looking for full-time or internship roles in Machine Learning Engineering, Data Analytics, or Business Intelligence — ideally on a team that values both rigorous analysis and the engineering discipline to put it into production.
